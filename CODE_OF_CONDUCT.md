# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity or expression, experience level, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, reviews, and discussions.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, and behavior rather than people.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and help resolve them.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption of project discussions or collaboration.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code,
issues, and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct.

Report a concern privately through a GitHub security advisory for this
repository. Reports will be handled with discretion and reviewed by project
maintainers.
